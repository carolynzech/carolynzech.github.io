I am a PhD student in the Foundations of Languages and Machines lab at MIT, advised by Rachit Nigam. My research focuses on type systems for hardware design.

Previously, I was a software engineer for the Kani model checker at Amazon.

I received my ScB in Computer Science from Brown University, where I developed a domain-specific language for enforcing software privacy policies as part of the ETOS group.
